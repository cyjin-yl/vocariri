## Candidate audit (useful regardless of visual routing)

Audit all text-domain candidates in the frozen forms ledger. Exclude scene-only
MMD/3D from text-form counts but preserve their useful background relationships.
Group the work into checkpoints of eight candidate forms, saving after each group.
For every form, trace cited observation IDs and source references. Compare mechanism,
target objects, hierarchy, temporal relationships and composition against other
forms. Mere color, font, duration or amplitude changes do not prove a distinct form.
Do not merge distinct masking, binding, grouping or reading-order relationships
because both labels say fade/scale/slide.

Write outputs/form-audit.jsonl with form_id, evidence IDs, closest neighbors,
mechanism distinction, missing evidence, art-design relevance, possible expressive
applicability and its limits, and proposed disposition: likely_distinct,
potential_duplicate, needs_native_review or unsupported_form_label. These are
proposals from existing textual evidence, not independent visual acceptance.
Do not fabricate a new observed form from an implementation idea. Preserve useful
art variations separately even when their underlying motion mechanism is shared.

Finish outputs/audit-summary.json with exact candidate count, resolved/dangling
references, proposal counts, unreviewed items and a small set of concrete native-frame
review requests ranked by uncertainty and architectural importance. Save a resumable
outputs/HANDOFF.md. Report which checks were performed, not just their intended result.
The objective is a finite audit of this snapshot, not fulfilling 500–800 forms now.
